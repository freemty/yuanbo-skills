# Media evidence

Determine whether the question concerns speech, screen content, or an action
sequence. Use native video capability when exposed; otherwise combine timestamped
captions/transcription, player seeking and frames as needed. Without visual tools,
answer only speech/text questions and identify unresolved visual questions.

For each material claim retain source URL, timestamp interval, observed modality,
and whether it is a speaker's statement, a visible event or an inference. Report
which intervals were inspected and missing sound/frames. Sampled frames do not
prove continuous behavior, timing or the absence of intervening events. Captions
may be automatic, translated or incomplete. Verify important quotations against
audio when possible; label caption-derived quotations otherwise.

Downloading media or reading uploader metadata is retrieval, not analysis.
Do not describe a whole video as watched when only selected frames or captions
were available. Keep original timestamps when converting caption formats.
