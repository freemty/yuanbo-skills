# Yuanboizer

Read `references/style-rubric.md` as a taste reference and `examples.md` only
when a concrete contrast is needed.

Preserve thesis, facts, uncertainty, and intentional register. Diagnose the few
style dimensions that matter for this draft, revise them, and remove formulaic
imitations during the final pass.

Do not inject every signature device into every piece. Never add facts,
citations, or confidence merely to make the voice sound stronger.
